Alpha Sigma Nu (ASN)

Alpha Sigma Nu is the only Jesuit Honors Society allowed to bear the name Jesuit. It teaches and recognizes students who hold the values of ASN: Loyalty, Scholarship, and Service. Its purpose is to recognize students of high academic achievement and provide a community for these students.

American Institute for Architecture Students

The American Institute of Architecture Students empowers students by fostering leadership, professional development, and advocacy through external partnerships with firms and other chapters in the Bay Area. We focus on growing our network with professionals and the American Institute of Architects.
